# Route 404 Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 20, 2026_

Route 404 is an offline maps and navigation app for Android and Android Auto. It is a personal fork of the open-source [CoMaps](https://codeberg.org/comaps/comaps) project, published by Nestware.

## Summary

Route 404 does not collect, store or share your personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

## Location

The app uses your device's location to show where you are, to plan routes and to give turn-by-turn directions. Location is processed only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

On builds installed from Google Play, the app may use Google Play services to get your location (the Fused Location Provider). That is handled by Google under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Data stored on your device

Your bookmarks, recorded tracks, search history, saved home and work places and settings are stored only on your device. You can delete them at any time in the app, or by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

## Network connections

The app connects to the internet only to:

- **Download and update offline maps** from CoMaps map servers (for example `cdn-fi-1.comaps.app`, `cdn-us-1.comaps.app` and their mirrors). These requests contain the map files being requested; like any web server, the map servers may see your IP address.
- **Edit OpenStreetMap**, only if you choose to sign in to an OpenStreetMap account in the app. Edits you submit are public and governed by the [OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).

Search, routing and navigation work offline on your device.

## Children

Route 404 is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
